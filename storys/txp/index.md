## Episode 1

Xen is a 21 year old skater girl. She loves pop culture and the general Tv Girl / grunge aesthetic.

One morning, Xen goes to her work place as usual – a small café chain that she used to visit as a child. She works as usual, making coffee and serving muffins to people.

It’s just a normal day again.

She eventually clocks out and heads home at 5:00PM. The sun is setting.

She skates home, gazing over the beautiful sunset, sitting nicely over the rooftops in the neighbourhood.

Until she comes across a particular street she hadn’t really noticed before.

Yet it feels familiar.

She rides down the street for a little while, until she sees a house. A relatively normal house. White painted walls, decent enough hedges surrounding a fairly well maintained garden.

Yet it makes her shudder.

Her skin crawls and the temperature seems to drop slightly.

She begins to turn away but bumps into someone.

‘sorry’

‘sorry'

Recognising the voice, Xen immediately looks up. Her mother walking away from her.

Xen immediately runs to her and gives her a hug from behind, as she realises and hugs back.

‘I never thought I’d see you again…’

## Episode 2

Xen is in her room listening to music. She's having a very peaceful time. At some stage, her friend texts her

'Hey I'm bouta go to the skate park, you free or na?'

Xen replies; 'I'll show up in some time, just relaxing for now'

She gets up and grabs some skating gear

As she heads to the park, she feels full of relief. She can't quite explain why. It's like something just started, while another is wrapping up.

It's almost like a dream

She meets her friend there, and they have a relaxing time skating together.

The sun begins to set.

They sit on top of a rather large ramp together, snacks in hand and laughing at some random bullshit.

he asks her; 'hey, it's been so nice knowing you. And for the past year I think I've developed a crush on you… will you…'

he gets flustered and nervous

'be my girlfriend?'

Xen is shocked. For the first time in a very long time, she's speechless.

her face flushes red.

'I… I don't know what to say… you're so nice to me and… and- YES!'

His face is filled with relief of a different kind.

They hug each other in a warm embrace, letting the moment sink in.

As evening turns to night, they part ways. Exchanging one last kiss goodbye.

Xen goes to her house, he goes to his.

At night, they can't stop thinking about each other. Texting each other and blushing at random intervals.

Xen's mum texts her,

'Goodnight, sweetie. Don't forget to visit me once in a while!'

at just about the same time has her boyfriend texts,

'Goodnight, beautiful. Can't wait to see you again <3'

Xen feels overstimulated from the overflow of huge events happening on 2 consecutive days. She takes the time to slow down, and rest in bed, before falling asleep.

Xen feels awake, but she's not sure if she really is. She sits up, and realises that she's not in her room. She tries to stand, but her legs aren't there.

She takes the blankets off her bed, only to realise that her legs are severed and pools of blood have seeped into the sheets.

She gags, nearly vomits, and stumbles to the floor.

A large sillhouette of a man holding a weapon of sorts stands in the doorframe

She can't make out what it is, or who...

the man leans down to her and whispers something she can't hear quite properly.

Xen looks at the man in confusion, but also pure terror.

The man then screams

WAKE!

Xen sits up in her bed suddenly.

Her legs are attached to her body.

The alarm clock reads, 7:30AM

'Shit, I'm late for work'
