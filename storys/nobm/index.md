# No One Believes Me by Xen

# Trigger Warning:

This story follows several triggering topics such as mental health issues, severe panic attacks and episodes, mentions of blood and gore etc.

## Episode 1

It’s staring at me. I’m staring at it.

There’s a pale ghost hanging upside down from the tree. It has straight black hair hanging over either side of it’s face. It’s smiling unnaturally wide, almost as if it has a Glasgow smile. It’s got void eyes, yet somehow I know it’s staring at me.

I wait patiently for it to move first. Yet it doesn’t move. It’s studying me or something.

Suddenly, it’s smile drops. Not even a split second after it starts crawling towards me. I shriek in horror as it begins to bang on my window. Once it realises it can’t get in, it bangs it’s head on the window instead. A crack begins to form. My heart races. It’s forehead starts bleeding. I nearly faint. It breaks through and crawls towards me.

It disintegrates.

‘Hey Kelly, you good?’

I turn to the now open door, breathing quickly, heart beating out of my chest. I slide down the wall I didn’t realise I was leaning against.

‘Another episode?’

I nod quickly, ‘y-yeah.’

‘Dude, you gotta start taking your meds again. No one just magically heals out of schizophrenia.’

## Episode 2

The following day was probably another hardest day of my life. I could barely stay awake for any of my classes. The entire day was spent staring at walls changing colours and people telling me I'm being dramatic and to just get over it. The instant my head landed on the table, I heard the loud smack of a ruler on my desk jolt me awake.

'Kelly! What did I tell you about sleeping in my class?' She yelled.

'S-sorry miss...' I mumbled, not caring to explain anymore.

Whenever I tried explaining people would just point and laugh or just not believe me. 'Excuses', they called it.

I don't think a giant demon ripping the roof off my house and terrorising me all night is an excuse. Even if it wasn't real.

I reluctantly sat up, trying to focus but my eyes constantly drifting off. Sometimes I'd flinch at the demons who tried to interact with me, or anything in general.

My heart sped up as I lost focus. I was still conscious, just was really panicky. Blood rushed to my head as the only thing keeping me alive was the hallucinations staring me dead in the eyes.

'Kelly?' My mate Harry tapped me on the shoulder, bringing me back to horrible reality, 'Dude, if you're feeling sick go home or soemthing.'

I stared at him for a second, trying to decide if he thought he knew me too well or just plain stupid.

'Harry, mate, we've been over this,' I audibly sighed, 'I'm having-'

He cut me off, saying 'a panic attack, yeah I get it mate. Everyone does at some point, go to the nurse.'

I just left without bothering to ask the teacher.

And that brings me to now. Sitting in the nurse's office waiting for something to happen.

---
I have to sit in the office for a while. Some kid that got in before me is rambling about a sore throat and how she must get home or she'll die. She won't die, she's faking to go home. Meanwhile I'm staring a facehugger dead in the eye as it assesses me; it's next meal.

Luckily enough, the moment it jumps at me is the moment the nurse decides to walk in.

I scream loud enough to cause an evacuation. The nurse jumps out of her skin.

'Jesus f- What the hell brought that on?!'

I stammer to find my english, 'The- uhh- the... thing... scared...'

'What thing?? There's nothing there..'

The girl from earlier walks past, glaring at me like I caused all of this. In fairness I kind of did.

'I'm schizophrenic... I saw a thing jump at me...'

'Listen, kid, you can't trick me. I know a faker when I see one.'

'Yeah well clearly not,' I mumble as I see the girl out the window walking out of the school and presumably home.

'Anyways, if you really have to get out of school so bad at least tell me why so I can get a teacher to authorise you. But only if you actually have a reason to leave.'

'Yeah well how about I'm seeing demons and I don't have time to deal with it while writing a page-long book report on bloody unicorns?'

'That's still fake... like I said you can't trick me with that'

I sigh and get up.

'H- hey, you can't just walk out!!'

'Watch me.'

I walk home.
