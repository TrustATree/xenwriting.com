# Sentimental by Xen

## Episode 1

The man at my door looked very sad. He was wearing an army outfit, like he too was a veteran.

'Sir, I've been sent here with bad news,' he said, 'your parents...'

I perked up

'They're dead.'

There was a long silence before he spoke;

'If you have anything to say... Or not...'

All I could do is hold back a smile.

'Well, feel free to...' he sighs, 'their funeral will be held this date and time, with further details enclosed' as he hands me a letter.

I take the letter with as straight of a face as I can manage and mumble something before heading back inside.

I look at the envelope for a second.

I place it neatly in a drawer of clothes I don't wear. I notice other things I was gifted. From friends I've since abandoned, my peers of which I haven't thought about in years...

And shut the drawer.

They mean nothing.

They're just items, they're not special.

Being a war veteran is hard... But being a psychopath is very much worse.

I hate it, but what more can I do?
