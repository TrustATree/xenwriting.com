## Episode 1

"Callie I swear to fucking god if you actually jump.."

My best friend yelled out in my direction, almost exactly up.

I'm sitting on the top of a 50 story building looking down from the edge, parachute in hand and a death wish. I couldn't hear her because I was too busy basking in the attention... and wind.

Once I gain enough confidence, I let go of the edge with my hands. I'l still sitting there. It looks a lot different now. It feels twice as high up, the wind feels stronger. Blood rushes to my head.

I don't want this to be more difficult.

I kick the wall, sending me falling off the edge.

* * *

I barely put my parachute on in time.

While falling I realised I hadn't put my parachute on. I panicked really fast, fumbled getting it on fast enough and pulled just slightly too late.

I hit the ground fast, breaking my leg in the process. It's a miracle I didn't break anything else.

In the hospital, all the nurses are looking at me either concerned or morbidly horrified. I couldn't give less of a shit, honestly.

I sit up, making everyone in the room wince. I don't feel pain. The amount of drugs they had me eat just numbed everything in my body.

I grin at my friend, Fiona.

She looks mildly grumpy.

"Come on, it was all shits and giggles." I exclaim. Not really exclaimed, but you get the idea. My teacher said I shouldn't use the word 'said'.

"More like shits and shatters.." She mumbled.

I have no idea how that relates to what I said so I ignore her.

She looks slightly more grumpy now.

I resort to watching the TV. The whatever show that was on finishes, and a show about stunt bikes comes on. The doctor quickly turns it off, causing me to pout.

He notices my pout but doesn't say anything.

* * *

Quick time skip because the rest of the hospital visit was boring as hell.

I'm let out a couple weeks later with my clothes and crutches. They didn't give me my parachute back.

The bus driver slows down when he sees me, but I wave him off and keep walking. Do you say walking when you have crutches? I dunno, it seems like the right word though.

I walk most of the way home until my brother, Harry, sees me walking and drags me inside.

"Jesus christ, Callie, did you walk all the way from the hospital with crutches?"

He looks actually concerned but I ignore it.

"Yeah what about it? It's not that far."

"It's seven fucking kilometers. I wouldn't walk that with two good legs."

I smirk at him, looking proud.

"Fuck are you grinning at." He snarls. I dunno why.

"Cos I walked that with one good leg."

He sighs, and drags me inside.

Mum looks mortified as Harry tells her what I did. I look unbothered as I turn on the Nintendo Wii and play Bowling.

She calls out "SIT DOWN, YOUNG LADY! YOU'VE GOT A BROKEN LEG AND I'M NOT FIXING IT!"

I sigh, and turn on the News instead (Bowling isn't fun if you're not standing up).

I'm on the news. They're talking about how I got out of the hospital after my 'Incredible leap of faith'.

I turn it off.
