## story (this isnt in episodes)

I can’t remember the events leading up to it. All that I can remember is I was wandering in the forest, until I reached a clearing. I set up camp in the clearing, cooked some marshmallows, let my guard down.

The next thing I remember, I was running for my life.

I never even saw the thing.

SASP employees found me, mostly unharmed, but tired. The ‘chase’ as I thought, was never truly a chase. Merely a warning. From the thing.

To never go back.

I never went back.

Never. Ever. Again.
