## Episode 1: The Book Beneath the Bedrock

Disclaimer: This story is not based on Minecraft Story Mode, but it is a Minecraft story
* * *

It began on a stormy night.

Jesse was repairing an old redstone machine in his underground workshop when his compass suddenly began spinning.

Then it stopped.

The needle pointed straight down.

"That's not physically possible," Jesse muttered.

The floor beneath his feet cracked open, revealing a hidden chamber. Inside sat a single chest made of blackstone, sealed with a golden lock.

Jesse opened it.

Inside was a book.

Its cover was made of crimson leather, and across the front were three words:

THE FINAL RESPAWN.

He opened the first page.

When the █████  ███ falls beyond the world's border, the ordinary laws of life and death shall not reach him.

Yet a soul may be called home through three fragments:

The Memory of the ████.

The Heart of the Nether.

The Crown of the Undying.

Bring them together at the First Altar, and the ████ ███████ may return.

Jesse read the page twice.

Then a third time.

"Guys," he said into his communicator. "Cancel whatever you're doing."

Flint's voice crackled through.

"If this is another redstone experiment, I'm not coming."

"I found a way to revive ███████████."

Silence.

Then Ash's voice:

"I'm getting my sword."

Iris spoke last.

"Jesse, if that book is genuine, someone has been hiding this knowledge for centuries."

"Then we'll find out who."

Jesse closed the book.

Their adventure had begun.

## Episode 2: The Memory of the ████

Their first destination was the Arena of Legends, the place where ███████████ had once fought enemies from across the Minecraft world.

The arena stood in a valley surrounded by mountains. 

Its walls had crumbled, its banners had turned to dust, and vines covered the entrance.

But the moment the four stepped inside, every torch ignited by itself.

A deep voice echoed through the arena.

"WHO DARES ENTER THE ████'S DOMAIN?"

Ash raised his shield.

"Something tells me we're not alone."

The floor shook.

From the opposite end of the arena emerged a gigantic armoured skeleton wearing a broken golden crown. 

It carried an enormous axe and had crimson light burning inside its eye sockets.

Iris recognised the runes engraved on its armour.

"That's a Guardian of the Final Altar! It must be protecting the first fragment."

The Guardian charged.

"SCATTER!" Jesse shouted.

Flint disappeared into the shadows and climbed the arena walls. 

Ash blocked the first axe strike, sliding backwards across the stone floor.

Iris raised her staff.

"AMETHYST BIND!"

Purple chains wrapped around the Guardian's legs.

"Jesse!" Ash yelled. "Any brilliant ideas?"

"Working on it!"

Jesse noticed redstone lamps arranged beneath the arena floor. 

They formed a circuit — an old combat mechanism.

He grinned.

"Flint! See those four levers?"

"Already there!"

"Pull them in the order I tell you!"

Jesse studied the Guardian's movements.

"LEFT! RIGHT! RIGHT! LEFT!"

Flint pulled the levers.

The entire arena lit up.

Pistons erupted from the walls, iron bars trapped the Guardian, and a massive anvil dropped from the ceiling onto its helmet.

The Guardian collapsed.

Ash stared at Jesse.

"You built an entire boss fight around a redstone puzzle?"

"Technically, the ancient builders built it. I just activated it."

"Of course you did."

Behind the Guardian's throne, they discovered a small crystal pulsing with red light.

The Memory of the ████.

Iris touched it with her staff.

Suddenly, the arena filled with a ghostly image of ███████████, standing victorious before a cheering crowd.

His voice echoed through the chamber.

"You think you can defeat me? I have a pickaxe, and I'll put it to good use."

The vision faded.

For the first time in years, the ruined arena sounded alive.

Flint quietly looked away.

"Right," he said. "Two more pieces to find."

But Shadow noticed something else.

At the bottom of the crystal, tiny words had appeared.

THE ████ IS NOT DEAD. HE IS WAITING.
